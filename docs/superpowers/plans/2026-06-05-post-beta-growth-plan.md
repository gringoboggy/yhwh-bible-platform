## ⛔ The inviolable guardrails (read first — apply to every phase)

These are non-negotiable; they're both the ethos AND the tactics (salesiness gets you ignored or banned in faith + scholarly rooms):

1. **NEVER mention donations in any outreach, post, or pitch.** Ko-fi / PayPal / Sponsors stay quietly on-site only and unlock nothing. Outreach that smells like a fundraiser is dead on arrival in these communities.
2. **The gift, not the pitch.** Everywhere the posture is *"here is a free thing that may serve you,"* never *"look what I built."* Plain, reverent, factual — never gaudy, salesy, or holier-than-thou.
3. **Doctrinal/textual humility is non-negotiable.** Position as a layperson offering a free tool, **never** an authority over the tradition. Credit every manuscript source scrupulously. Invite correction openly (the mailto). Treat the first error a priest or scholar flags as a **gift** — fix it fast.
4. **Warm contacts are relationships, not broadcast channels.** Beta maṣāḥǝft, HMML, AGADE, Mahibere Kidusan get **one** careful message each, then you let them act. Follow-up spam / marketing tone burns a contact you can't re-earn.
5. **Match the hook to the room.** Heritage/rarity for scholars + the devout core; useful-free-tool (copy-paste, no lock-in, deepest free apparatus) for laity + Bible-app users; open-source + rare-data for the tech moment. Never pitch "EPUB builder" to scholars or "rare manuscript" to app users.
6. **Don't grind.** No daily posting, no Telegram/WhatsApp blasting, no content treadmill — unsustainable for a solo maker and reads as spam. Favor passive/compounding assets + a few high-leverage one-shots.
7. **Be present for the one-shots.** Show HN + big subreddit posts need *hours* of calm replies — book the day. A fire-and-forget post underperforms and reads as drive-by promo. Don't fire Show HN until the faith foundation is real and QA is closed.
8. **Respect each community's rules; verify before claiming.** Read every sub's self-promo rule, flair correctly, be transparent it's yours. Never quote unverified Facebook/Telegram group sizes — visit and verify first. A pin/post may assert only what HAS shipped, never what hasn't.

## The one strategic fact that shapes everything: TWO funnels, not one

- **Funnel A — the faithful + heritage circles** (Ethiopian/Eritrean Orthodox Tewahedo + Geʽez/manuscript scholars). They live on **Telegram / Facebook / YouTube / academic mailing-lists** — *not* Reddit/HN. They are who the reader is genuinely *for*, and their word-of-mouth carries real weight. **Serve them first.**
- **Funnel B — open-source / digital-humanities / Bible-tech** (GitHub / Hacker News / awesome-lists / search). Served by **passive compounding assets** (search + lists) that accrue quietly underneath, plus **one** deferred, program-framed tech moment.

A tactic that wins in A is invisible or harmful in B, and vice-versa. **Hero asset = the rare manuscript reader, not the builder** — heritage/rarity travels further than software-feature copy. Lead with the reader everywhere; the builder is the quieter "and there's also a free tool."

**Hero hook (the one line):** *"Likely the only free, manuscript-sourced Geʽez + English Bible you can actually read AND copy-paste — transcribed from the original manuscripts, free forever. (And there's also a free, open-source tool to build your own customizable Ethiopian Tewahedo study Bible.)"*

**Primary audience (decided):** the Ethiopian/Eritrean Orthodox Tewahedo faithful (diaspora + homeland) + the small Geʽez/manuscript heritage circles around them. Open-source/Bible-tech is the secondary funnel via passive assets + one deferred tech moment.

## Metrics (the few worth watching)

- **Leading — warm-intro replies:** did Beta maṣāḥǝft / HMML / a Digital Orientalist editor / Mahibere Kidusan actually respond? (1–2 genuine replies in 6 weeks = success at this scale.)
- **Leading — corrections received via the mailto:** the right devout/scholarly people are reading closely (each fix = a trust win).
- **Leading — durable assets placed:** live Wikipedia citations, accepted Wikisource contributions, approved faith.tools/`christian_foss`/CrossWire listings (~5–8 compounding assets in month 1).
- **Lagging — reader engagement:** visits to `/read/*` + Copy-chapter-button uses (the gift is being read + copied, not just landed on).
- **Lagging — organic search** to the explainer pages (the cleanest signal the SEO graft is compounding).
- **Lagging — GitHub stars/forks + inbound issues** on the program repo (funnel-B interest).
- **Lagging — referral sources** in analytics (which channel is actually working).
- **Truest — return-visit rate + unsolicited word-of-mouth** (a creator referencing it, repeat readers): for a trust-based community, depth beats any one-time spike.

> Analytics note: the site is no-tracking by design. Pick a privacy-respecting, cookieless measure (e.g. a self-hosted/lightweight counter, or GitHub/referrer + Giscus signals) — never anything that compromises the "no tracking" promise. Decide this in Phase 0.

## Why this order (synthesis logic)

Passive/compounding assets **first** (no presence needed, and they legitimize you) → warm amplifying intros **second** (they spread it free, but need credibility to land) → faith core **third** (patient relationships, the real long-term audience) → the one tech moment **last** (highest variance; needs the site to reward scrutiny + a real foundation so it doesn't read as drive-by promo). The SEO/content engine isn't a separate phase — it's grafted as a free byproduct of the About + explainer pages (Phase 0) and the guest post (Phase 2), so durable search traffic accrues with zero extra grind.
