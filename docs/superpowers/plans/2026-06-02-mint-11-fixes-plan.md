## Executive summary

Round 4 surfaced 64 deduped findings, of which 30 survived adversarial verification (34 refuted). By severity the survivors break down as 13 medium, 14 low, and 3 info; 24 are bug/correctness/security/debt/test/doc findings and 6 are optimizations. The most serious cluster is a set of medium correctness defects that can produce wrong or negative annotation counts on published pages, silently drop a book's entire note corpus on a parse failure, write live corpus translation stores without atomic-write protection, and leave 7 stale tests passing green against shipped Ge'ez marathon books (masking real regressions).
