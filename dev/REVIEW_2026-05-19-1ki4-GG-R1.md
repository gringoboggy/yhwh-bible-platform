## Cross-glyph question: `❈` (U+2748) vs `✣` (U+2723)

- **VERDICT: distinct glyphs, BUT implementer over-distinguished them in the body.**
- Two glyph classes ARE distinct on this parchment:
  1. **Large knot/figure-8 cross** in RED — appears immediately after `ክፍል ፴`, `ክፍል ፴ ወ ፪`, `ክፍል ፴ ወ ፫`, and `ክፍል ፴ ወ ፩` rubrics. These are the rubric companions. They are the size of a large display letter and use the figure-8 / interlace stroke pattern. These genuinely correspond to `❈` U+2748.
  2. **Small 4-armed cross** with red dot flanking at top/bottom — used throughout the body for clause/phrase division. These correspond to `✣` U+2723.
- The implementer's claim that `❈` appears 3 times "at 3 major narrative breaks" inside the body geez is **REJECTED**. The 3 places they marked `❈` (v8 between `ምዕራ` and `ወዝንቱ`, v14 at end of `በይሰክር`, v23 at end of `መርዶ`) are all the same small `✣` glyph as elsewhere. The genuine large `❈` ONLY appears with the four `ክፍል <numeral>` rubrics, which the implementer correctly excluded from body text. So the body should contain only `✣`; the three body-text `❈` instances in the JSON should be normalised to `✣`. The shipped `validate_witness` whitelist for `✣` then covers everything.
