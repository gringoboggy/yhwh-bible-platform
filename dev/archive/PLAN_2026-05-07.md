## Sequence forward

The platform is functionally complete for the buyer demo *except
for corpus depth*. Sequence puts cheap debt-closing first, then
the big corpus push, then polish.

### Tier 1 — Close cheap debt (foundational, low risk)

```
ω.3  API reference page (/api/help console)        ~1 hr
     Auto-generated enumeration of every /api/* route.
     Helps future Claude / future dev orient. Can scan
     the route table in scripts/web.py since the routes
     follow a consistent pattern.
     Risk: LOW (read-only)   Console: 13

★    web.py split into scripts/templates/<name>.py  ~1.5 hr
     Move the 13 *_HTML constants (61% of web.py's 12,784
     lines) into separate template modules. web.py
     re-exports for back-compat. Update bulk_inject and
     scaffold_console.py to scan the templates/ directory.
     Risk: MEDIUM   Files: scripts/web.py, scripts/templates/*,
                            scripts/bulk_inject.py,
                            scripts/scaffold_console.py
     Do BEFORE adding more consoles — the deeper the file
     gets, the harder this becomes.
```
