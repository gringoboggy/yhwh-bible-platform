### M-3. v17 — `ክፍል ፪` rubric absorbed as verse tokens

C-2 v17 has `ክፍል ፪ ✣ ወመጽአ ብእሴ እግዚአብሐር…` as the verse, with `ክፍል`, `፪`, `✣` as the first three tokens.

This is a versification design choice: should liturgical sub-rubrics be INSIDE the verse string or surface as separate `meta_rubric` annotations? The Ethiopic chapter division uses `ምዕራፍ N` (chapter) and `ክፍል N` (section) — the section markers are not part of the verse text proper. They are scribal/liturgical metadata.

**Decision needed (likely defer to C-7 spec):** either keep current treatment consistently across all chapters, or split sub-rubrics out of the verse text. Currently v17, v24 (`ክፍል ፫`), v25 (`ክፍል ፬`) all share this pattern.
