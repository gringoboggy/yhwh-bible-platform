## Executive summary

Round 10 surfaced **44 verified survivors** (of 64 deduped findings; 20 refuted), partitioned as **34 bug/correctness/security/debt/test/doc findings** and **10 optimization recommendations**. By severity: **1 high, 12 medium, 23 low, 8 info**. The single high is a real silent-data-loss defect — the frozen desktop app's `content_root()` resolves to the read-only/ephemeral PyInstaller bundle, so in-app note edits write into `_MEIPASS` and are lost on exit (or blocked under macOS Gatekeeper). The remaining medium tier is dominated by red CI guards (lint/cache-audit/stale-pins on the shipped build engine) and reproducibility/byte-stability gaps (unhashed default theme CSS; wall-clock timestamps in the Kindle re-zip). Overall the codebase is **healthy and shippable** (v0.1.0 is out): no data is currently corrupt, the 9 KJV editions are byte-stable, and most findings are defense-in-depth, latent-future-update traps, or stale tests/docs lagging deliberate config flips — but the high and the two byte-stability findings are genuine ship-quality defects that warrant prompt local-commit fixes.
