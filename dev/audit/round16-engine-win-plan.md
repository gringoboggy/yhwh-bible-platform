## Executive summary

Round-16 deep-audit produced **36 deduped findings → 26 verified survivors / 10 refuted** (of the 26 survivors, **0 are UNVERIFIED**; every one cleared an adversarial skeptic panel). Severity split is **{medium: 3, low: 23}**; all 26 are bug/correctness/security/debt/test/doc — **0 optimization** recommendations. The most serious are the three mediums: (a) the core config loaders decode glyph-bearing YAML with the platform-default encoding, a hard cp1252 crash on Windows whenever the app/CLI runs without `PYTHONUTF8`; (b) the study-glossary str splitter still holds ~3× copies of the ~480 MB monolith and its structure-surprise fallback re-decodes the whole file, reintroducing the flagship-eink OOM the byte-streamer was built to prevent; (c) the round-16 headline display-redundancy gate scans for a class the build never emits and omits the body/byline checks its own docstring promises — a dead gate. Overall codebase health is **good**: no live data-loss or shipped-output defects, the 9 KJV editions remain byte-stable, and most survivors are latent-until-future-data, audit-script gaps, orphan UI options, or OS-packaging parity nits. Several survivors are duplicate framings of two underlying issues (the `computer` orphan target, ×3; `verse_marker_glyph` orphan, ×2) — consolidated below without altering the verified count.
