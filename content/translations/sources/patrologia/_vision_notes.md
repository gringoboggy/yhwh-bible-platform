## PO Esther page layout (calibrated 2026-05-28 by rendering pp.24/32/35/37/48/60 + 230-DPI body strips)
Each page, top → bottom:
1. **French banner** — `LE LIVRE D'ESTHER. — <Roman ch>, <verse-range>` (or addition form `… — B, 1`); bracketed page-number at the corners. Tells the chapter/addition + verse span on that page.
2. **Ge'ez BODY** (larger type; ~top 6–42% of the page) — the scripture. **Verse numbers run down the margins** (left and/or right) AND as small inline superscripts; Additions carry a **letter label** in the margin (e.g. `B. 1`).
3. **Apparatus band** (smaller, dense Ge'ez; ~42–60%) — the editor's manuscript-variant readings, keyed to verse-number + manuscript **sigla (M / N / O / P / Q …)**. This is **editorial, NOT scripture — EXCLUDE it.**
4. **French translation** (bottom ~40%).

**Legibility = GO.** At 230 DPI / ≤1568px the body glyphs + margin numerals are clearly readable. The apparatus band is visually separable (smaller type, below the body) — a vision agent can transcribe the body + capture the margin numbers + exclude the apparatus, where the earlier Tesseract OCR could not (the OCR swept the Ge'ez-script apparatus into the verse text + lost the margin numerals → the current KJV-renumbered garbage `est_patrologia.py`, e.g. vv1:4–1:6 are almost pure apparatus).

**Render the body strip for vision** = top **0.45** of the page (banner + body + margins; apparatus + French excluded), DPI **230** (full-width ≈ 1518px, under the vision downsample cap). Reuse `extract_patrologia_pdf._render_strip_to_png` / the new `render_body_for_vision`.

## LOCKED decision (a) — Additions store-encoding
**Canonical chapters stay int (1–10). Each Addition's verses live INSIDE its host canonical chapter, at the source position where Pereira prints them, with STRING verse labels** (`"A1".."A17"`, `"B1".."B7"`, `"C1"…`, `"D1"…`, `"E1"…`, `"F1"…`).
- e.g. Esther chapter 3's stored `VERSES` (source order): `(3,1)…(3,13), (3,"B1")…(3,"B7"), (3,14), (3,15)`.
- Addition A → leading verses `(1,"A1")…(1,"A17")` before `(1,1)`. F → trailing verses in chapter 10. C+D → chapter 4 trailing (after 4:17), in C-then-D order.

**Why this is correct + safe (verified against `scripts/build_standalone.py`):**
- The renderer does `for ch in sorted(by_ch)` (build_standalone.py:252) — keeping **canonical chapters int** means `sorted()` never sees a mixed int/str chapter set ⇒ no `TypeError`, no clumping.
- The renderer **preserves verse source-order within a chapter** (`chapter_verses_in_source_order` :183; `render_chapter_body` does NOT re-sort verses) ⇒ the addition renders in its exact interleaved position (B between 3:13 and 3:14) — **faithful to Pereira**.
- `render_chapter_body` (:93–105) only **string-interpolates + dict-keys** the verse number (`seen[gv]`, `f"…{gv}…"`, `appmap.get(str(gv))`, `en_map.get((gv,occ))`) — it **never does int arithmetic on the verse** ⇒ **string verse labels are fully tolerated** (verified by reading the function).
- **Byte-stable for Kings/Samuel/Psalms:** they have only int verses → behavior unchanged.
- **No renderer surgery.** Rejected alternatives: string CHAPTER keys (break `sorted()`); int-offset addition chapters A=101…F=106 (clump additions after ch10, losing the interleave); continuous int verse renumber (collides with / shifts canonical verse numbers → breaks KJV xref + violates "trust the source numbering").

## Recurring vision failure-classes (append during Task-4 transcription)
- **(a) Title/opening pages run TALLER than the body default.** On p24 the body (title + A1–A5) runs to **~0.58 of page height**, so the calibrated `geez_top_fraction=0.45` strip **truncates** it (cuts off the lower verses). → For opening/title pages render the body strip at **≥0.55** (p24 = 1518×1409 at 0.58, still **≤1568px**, under the vision downsample cap). Plain interior pages keep 0.45.
- **(b) Verses can SPLIT across a page boundary.** A5 on p24 ends **mid-clause at the right page edge** — last visible glyph is a bare `ከ` with **no `።` terminator** — and continues on p25. → The transcript flags it `_partial=true` + `_continues_on_page`; the **Task-4 assembly step must concatenate the split parts** and **never emit a partial verse as complete**. (`last_verse_complete=false` is the per-page signal.)
- **(c) Apparatus boundary on p24 = a CENTERED Ge'ez gloss line BEFORE the siglum-keyed rows.** Below the 7 body lines there is one **centered** smaller-type Ge'ez line (opens roughly `A. 1. በካልእ …` — it re-quotes the lemma being annotated), then the **siglum-keyed `M / N / O / P / Q` variant rows + French**. → **Exclude everything from that centered gloss line downward.** Also: the **small raised star-markers at word-starts** in the body (seen before A1, A2, A3, A4, A5 openings) are **footnote reference marks, NOT verse numerals** — verse numbers are the margin Arabic/letter labels only.
- **(d) Low-contrast glyph pairs caught on p24 (note the actual pairs):**
  - **qi vs qē** — A1 Kish proper noun: correct is **5th-order `ቄ`** (`ዘቄስዩ`), not 2nd-order `ቂ` (`ዘቂስዩ`).
  - **2nd-order ʾu vs 4th-order ʾä** — A5 verb "came": correct is **4th-order `አ`** singular (`መጽአ`), not 2nd-order `ኡ`/`ʾu`.
  - **6th-order wə vs 1st-order wä** — A2 "he/it-is": printed as **1st-order `ወ`** (`ወእቱ`) where 6th-order `ው` (`ውእቱ`) is expected (see (e); kept as printed + flagged).
- **(e) As-printed oddities are NOT errors — preserve + flag, do not "correct" toward the expected form:**
  - **A2** "he/it-is" `ወእቱ` (1st-order wä) vs expected `ውእቱ` (6th-order) — flagged uncertain.
  - **A3** "captivity / was-carried-captive" printed with **gi/ge** (`ጊዋ`, `ዘጌወወ`), not ṗē.
  - **A4** opening word `ወገዘንቱ` (wä-**gä**-zän-tu, an **extra ga**) vs expected `ወዘንቱ` (wä-zəntu).
- **(f) Verses can split mid-WORD across a page boundary — and the low-contrast 1st/6th-order pairs bite exactly at the break.** A5 ends p24 at a cut word `…መጽአ ፡ ክ` (6th-order **ክ**, NOT 1st-order ከ) and completes at the top of p25 as `ልኤሆሙ ፡ ይትጋደሉ ፡ ወከነ ፡ ቃሎሙ ፡ ዐቢይ ።` → the joined break-word is **`ክልኤሆሙ`** ("both of them" = ክልኤ + ሆሙ). The first p25 pass misread the break as 1st-order ከ/ለ and mis-segmented `ለኤሆሙ…ዐቢይ።` as "A6" — but it is **A5's tail**; the real **A6 begins at `ወበቀለ`**. **Lesson:** at every page start, FIRST test whether the opening word(s) complete the previous page's cut verse (join the cut glyph; check the ከ/ክ + ለ/ል 1st-vs-6th-order pairs) BEFORE assigning the first margin number — otherwise every verse on the page is off by the tail.
- **(g) Verse-delimiting signals differ PAGE TO PAGE — read each page's actual marks.** p24 carries **in-text Geʽez numerals** at verse starts (`።። ፪ወይነብር` … `።። ፭ወናሁ`). p25 has **NONE** — the only in-text Geʽez figure on it is the *cardinal* `፪` ("two eunuchs") inside the running text; p25's verses are signalled only by the `።` stops + the **left-margin Arabic numbers**, which here index **physical body LINES** (the apparatus is keyed to the same 6.–13. line numbers). The `✻` stars at word-starts are **footnote-reference marks, NOT verse boundaries** (confirms/sharpens (c)). Do not assume one numbering scheme across pages.
- **(h) A single transcriber + one adversarial reviewer is NOT sufficient for this faint hand-set print — a CONVERGENCE step is required.** Two independent Opus passes on p25 diverged on ~10 glyphs, including the **divine-name order `እግዚአብሔር` vs `እግዚአብሐር` (ሔ/ሐ)** and `በሌሊት` ("at night") vs `በሰሊጥ`, *and* the first "APPROVE CLEAN" reviewer missed the (f) segmentation error entirely. → Per page: ≥2 independent transcriptions → **adjudicate disagreements glyph-by-glyph at high zoom** → flag genuinely-ambiguous loci as `uncertain` (never silently pick one). Budget this across the 40 PO Esther pages (+ the 5 later PO books).

- **(i) Read reference material FIRST to read the print better — this is CALIBRATION, not harmonizing (user-directed 2026-05-29).** Before/while transcribing, spend time reading anything that helps you read the actual printed glyphs + understand the Geʽez usage: the SAME edition's OTHER pages (learn the typesetter's orthographic/type habits — which vowel-orders the fount visually distinguishes), the editor's OWN translation band (here Pereira's French — confirms a word's MEANING, hence its identity), and grammars/lexica. The line: learning HOW TO READ the print = good (calibration); changing WHAT IS PRINTED to match a standard Bible = forbidden (harmonizing). On p25 an adjudicator talked itself out of checking sibling pages for the tsade habit by mislabeling it "harmonizing" and left a resolvable glyph flagged uncertain — that self-restriction was WRONG. Budget a short up-front calibration read per book/edition; it pays off across all its pages.
- **(j) PO-9 Esther (Pereira) tsade type-habit — CALIBRATED edition-wide (p25 + pp.24/30/40/50/60).** The fount marks ONLY the 6th-order ጽ (a short descending vertical leg below the base); 1st-order ጸ is the flat two-loop base with no leg. It shows NO visually-distinct 4th-order ጻ — "righteous" (ṣadəq) prints with the 1st-order flat ጸ base, NOT ጻ. Decide ጸ vs ጽ by presence/absence of the descending leg; do NOT expect a distinct ጻ and do NOT "correct" toward standard orthography. Decisive same-line minimal pair (p25 A12): ዐጸድ "court" (flat ጸ) immediately before ኃጽዋኒሁ "eunuchs" (descending-leg ጽ).

- **(k) The edition uses a ግ/ገ-onset demonstrative ("this") in SOME positions but plain ዝንቱ in others — read each occurrence, do NOT unify.** p26 confirms the p24 A4 `ወገዘንቱ` oddity is systematic: A15 `ለገዝንቱ` (×2) + A16 `ግዝንቱ` carry the ገ/ግ-onset (B + adjudicator + controller crops agree; cf. p24 A4), YET the canonical heads `ወእምድኅረ ፡ ዝንቱ ፡ ነገር` (1:1, 1:4) are plain ዝንቱ (both blind passes + a controller crop). Record per-occurrence + flag; do NOT force page-wide consistency (a 1913 hand-set fount is legitimately inconsistent).
- **(l) Crash-recovery convergence works — recover the transcripts, don't redo the heavy vision.** p26's heavy passes (2 blind transcribers + 1 adjudicator) finished just before an OOM crash; their subagent transcripts (`…/<session-id>/subagents/agent-*.jsonl`) preserved the full transcriptions + the French-anchored adjudication. The page was finished by a CONTROLLER re-verification pass (high-zoom crops of ONLY the contested glyphs) — no re-running the heavy vision. Method: pull each agent's assistant text blocks from the jsonl (PowerShell `ConvertFrom-Json` per line → collect `message.content[].text`), reconcile A-vs-B vs adjudicator, re-verify the divergences against fresh crops, write + Ethiopic-only codepoint-gate.
- **(m) The adjudicator can OVER-APPLY a glyph pattern — verify its deviations against the two blind passes.** On p26 the adjudicator correctly caught the ገ-onset demonstrative but OVER-EXTENDED ግ: it read A15 `ተዝካረ` ('record/memorial') as `ተገክረ` and the plain 1:1/1:4 heads as `ግዝንቱ` — both contradicted by BOTH blind transcribers (+ controller crops). **Rule: where the adjudicator deviates from a reading the two blind passes AGREE on, re-verify with a fresh high-zoom crop before accepting.** The load-bearing French-anchored calls (numeral 127, the long A17) stood; only the unflagged ግ-extensions needed correction.
