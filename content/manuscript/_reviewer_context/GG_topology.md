## 1. Hand description

GG-00106 is the **Gunda Gundē "all-in-one historical-books" manuscript** (University of Toronto Scarborough Gunda Gundē Digital Library, bot-walled — supplied as user-cropped images). The hand is a careful three-column Ethiopic **book hand** with red rubrication. Inks are generally clean and legible at high magnification; chapters do not normally span more than 2–3 folios.

The hand registers TWO body cross-glyphs:
- `✣` (U+2723, small four-armed cross) — used inline throughout body geez for clause / phrase division. Red but small.
- `❈` (U+2748, large knot / figure-8 interlace cross) — used ONLY immediately after the red `ክፍል N` rubrics that mark fine liturgical subdivisions. NEVER inside body geez.

**Body geez contains ONLY `✣`.** Any `❈` in a transcribed body geez string is a transcriber over-distinction error (the small + large crosses look superficially similar at low zoom). At C-3, normalize any body `❈` token to `✣`.

## 2. Stroke-pattern references for confused fidel families

The following fidels are the documented systematic-failure classes for this hand. Verify EVERY instance of each in proper nouns (`✪` high risk) and verb prefixes (`◎` medium risk) before approving. Use 16x–40x crops; if the call is genuinely at the resolution limit, flag AMBIGUOUS-PARCHMENT rather than guess.

### `ለ` (lā) vs `ስ` (sā) — `ለ`/`ስ` family ✪

- `ለ` (la, 1st-order): broad-topped with a curved right hook; like a wide saucer. The top stroke arcs across the full width.
- `ስ` (sa, 1st-order): narrower top with a vertical descent on the right; less arc, more angle. Center-stroke is straighter.

**Pattern.** Where ambient text suggests a Ge'ez form starting `እለ` (relative pronoun "those who") or containing `ለ` in toponyms (`ጋላዓድ` Gilead, `ፈለግ` river), the transcriber routinely writes `እስ` / `ሳ` / `ፈስግ`. Re-verify every word-initial / mid-word `ስ` at 20x+; many are `ለ`.

**Confirmed instances** (kept for stroke calibration):
- 1Ki4 GG f030r-R `ፈለግ` (river) was transcribed `ፈስግ` in R1; corrected to `ፈለግ` after parchment re-read at 30x.
- 1Ki4 GG `እለ ይስሕቡ` ("those who pull") was transcribed `እስ ይስሕቡ`; same family.
- 1Ki4 GG `ጋላዓድ` (Gilead) was transcribed `ጋሳዓድ` then `ገሳዓድ`; corrected to `ጋላዓድ`.

### `ይ` (yə) vs `ደ` (də) — `ይ`/`ደ` family ✪

- `ይ` (yə, 6th-order): a hooked vertical with a top-right curl, no foot. Open form.
- `ደ` (də, 1st-order): a fuller body with a closed bowl on the right and a clear baseline foot.

**Pattern.** Where ambient text suggests a standard Ge'ez `ይ`-prefixed verb (`ይበል`, `ይትገበር`, `ይሰርዑ`, `ይትቀንዩ`, `ይከውን`, `ይስምዑ`) or the proper noun `ይሁዳ` (Judah), the transcriber writes `ደ`. Same direction also occurs in reverse on REGNAL chapters where `ደ`-letters in name spellings get written as `ይ`. Re-verify every word-initial `ደ` or verb-prefix-position `ደ` against the printed-Bible expected `ይ`.

**Confirmed instances:**
- 1Ki4 GG `ይሁዳ` (Judah) was repeatedly transcribed `ደሁዳ`; corrected.
- 1Ki4 GG `ይበልዑ` ("they eat") was `ደበልዑ`; same family.
- 1Ki4 GG `ሕይወቱ` ("his life") was `ሕደወቱ`; same family.

### `ያ` (yā) vs `ደ` (də) — `ያ`/`ደ` final-syllable family ✪

- `ያ` (yā, 4th-order): a hooked vertical with a horizontal stroke pulling down-right; an "open" descender.
- `ደ` (də, 1st-order): same as above (closed bowl, foot).

**Pattern.** In LAST syllables of proper-noun/deputy/king names, `ያ` is systematically written `ደ`. Re-verify every word-final `ደ` in person/place names.

**Confirmed instances:**
- 1Ki4 GG deputy names `አኪያድ`, `አኪያል`, `አርንያ` were transcribed `አኪደድ`, `አኪደል`, `አራንደ`; all corrected.

### `ሰ` (sa, 1st-order) vs `ስ` (s, 6th-order) — `ሰ`/`ስ` Solomon-name family ◎

- `ሰ` (sa, 1st-order): broad top, NO foot, NO 6th-order descender curl. Looks like a wide hat.
- `ስ` (s, 6th-order): narrower top, with a small descending curl on the bottom-right.

**Pattern.** The opening of "Solomon" (`ሰሎምን` or `ስሎምን`) is ambiguous between the two scribal habits depending on register. The 1Ki4 v1 reference confirms `ወሰሎምን` (1st-order `ሰ`); other Solomon instances in the chapter may legitimately differ.

### `ዐ` vs `አ` consonant family ◎

- `ዐ` (ʿə): distinctive bell-shaped left flank.
- `አ` (ʾə): cleaner triangular shape.

**Pattern.** Same-direction confusion possible in both directions. Re-verify at name positions and in `ዓ`/`አ` toponym distinctions (`ጋዛ`/`ጋዘ`).

### Vowel-mark micro-confusions ◎

The following vowel-mark pairs share visual similarity and the transcriber routinely smooths toward printed-Bible forms. None are individually high-risk, but they accumulate; the C-3 reviewer should spot-check every chapter:

- `ቤ`/`በ` (be / ba)
- `ፉ`/`ፋ` (fu / fa)
- `ዘ`/`ዛ` (ze / za)
- `ሐ`/`ሕ` (1st / 6th of ḥ)
- `ኆ`/`ኁ` (ḫo / ḫu)
- `ሎ`/`ሉ` (lo / lu)
- `ቡ`/`ቦ` (bu / bo) — final-syllable plural/singular distinction
- `ያ`/`ዐ` (yā / ʕə) — only outside the `ያ`/`ደ` family

## 3. Numeral references

Ethiopic numerals U+1369–U+137C (`፩` 1 through `፼` 10,000) are STAND-ALONE tokens. The `_geez_to_tokens` regex `_NUMERAL_RE` inserts spaces around them.

**Red glyph appearing at a count position is a NUMERAL, never the fidel letter `ቯ` (qwā).** The thousand-suffix mark `ሺ` (often appearing as a small red multiplier after a digit) is a numeral. `ቯ` does not appear in Kings body geez.

**Confirmed instances:**
- 1Ki4 GG v23, v26 — multiple red Ethiopic numerals (the 40,000 stalls / 12,000 horsemen / 3,000 parables / 1,005 songs sequences) initially transcribed `ቯ ቯ ኤፍራስ` etc.; corrected to `፬ ሺ ኤምሳለ` etc.

## 4. Cross-column / cross-folio boundary references

**METHOD NOTE 2 (column-boundary scripture omission)** is the worst defect class. Every claimed recensional minus / short reading must be independently verified at the boundary, glyph-by-glyph. A word routinely splits across the turn so a verse boundary may NEVER fall mid-word.

**Known clean boundaries** (kept as worked examples for the boundary-trace technique):
- 1Ki4 GG f030v-L L41 → f030v-M L1: word `ወአልሕምት` splits as `ወአ | ልሕምት` (or `ወአል | ሕምት` — split point ambiguous at the smudgy edge but reconstruction is clean either way).
- 1Ki4 GG `ሳዕ | ለ` (line-break, not column-break) was misread as `ሳቦሉ` in R1 — same class of failure WITHIN a column. The boundary-trace check applies to every line break, not just column breaks.

## 5. Resolution-limited (AMBIGUOUS-PARCHMENT) classes

These classes cannot be resolved at the available image resolution (~4500 DPI equivalent from the supplied JPG crops). The reviewer flags AMBIGUOUS-PARCHMENT and defers to CAM cross-reference at C-7 collation OR (rarely) to a higher-res CUDL pull.

- **Rubric-vs-tinted-`:` axis at inter-word boundaries.** Small red marks at inter-word positions may be either a `✣` rubric or a tinted `:` separator. Discriminated only at 30x+ with explicit color separation; the discriminating evidence is at the limit of available zoom. (Surfaced by 1Ki4 R7 at v5 mid + v9 tail.)
- **Sub-glyph orthographic uncertainty** (vowel-mark micro-confusions in clean text where both readings are plausible) — ACCEPTABLE; honestly-flag, do not push toward printed spelling (re-introduces harmonization).
- **`ቯ`-look-alike red glyphs** in 1Ki4 v23, v26 numeral positions — resolved as numerals (per §3), but the exact digit value is resolution-limited (`፬ ሺ` vs `፰ ሺ` etc.).
