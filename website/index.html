How to read your download

Every edition is a standard EPUB — the universal, DRM-free ebook format. Open it on:

  • iPhone / iPad / Mac — tap the file → Open in Books (Apple Books).
  • Kobo — drag the EPUB onto the Kobo app, or send it to your e-reader.
  • Android — open in Kobo or any EPUB reader.
  • Desktop — Apple Books, Calibre, or Thorium Reader.
