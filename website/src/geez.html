The sources we transcribe from

Every reading is taken from real manuscripts and scholarly editions you can see for yourself:

  • Cambridge University Library — MS Add. 1570 — Geʽez Octateuch & Samuel/Kings, in high-resolution IIIF folios.
  • Patrologia Orientalis — printed critical Geʽez editions.
  • The HaCohen Geʽez apocrypha (Wisdom, Sirach) — clean printed text.

What further support makes possible

The program and every Bible it builds are free. Support is optional and doesn’t unlock anything — it funds the transcription work: more witnesses collated, more books completed, and the Amharic started.
