The sources

Every reading is taken from real manuscripts and scholarly editions you can see for yourself — chiefly Cambridge MS Add. 1570 and the printed critical editions of the Patrologia Orientalis. The full source list and the transcription method are on the about page.

What further support makes possible

The program and every Bible it builds are free. Support is optional and doesn’t unlock anything — it funds the transcription work: more witnesses collated, more books completed, and the Amharic started.
