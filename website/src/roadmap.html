The road so far — and ahead

Where the work stands. There are no dates and no promises here — just what’s done, what’s being worked on, and what we’d like to add later. The program is free at whatever stage it has reached; nothing here is held back behind a price, and nothing here is owed to you on a deadline.

Development stages

  1. Shipped

    The storehouse & study apparatus

    One 83-book Ethiopian Tewahedo superset — the broadest canon still in use — with 91,553 study notes gathered into one place and organized into families you can turn on or off. A large set of free study material, stored once, in plain text.

  2. Shipped

    The customizable Bible builder

    Choose your canon and tradition, toggle the notes you want, add original-language verse popups (Hebrew, Greek, Latin, and Arabic), design your cover and title pages, and export a clean EPUB 3 — validated to zero errors and zero warnings. Nine starting editions build today, entirely on your own computer.

  3. Shipped

    Customization down to the verse

    Choosing which notes and original-language popups appear at every level of the Bible — the whole, a book, a chapter, or a single verse. The builder’s navigator lets you walk your edition like a Bible and tune it anywhere, with the most specific choice winning. Live now.

  4. Shipped

    Your cover, and a Bible that tells the truth about itself

    Twenty-five cover designs — five styles in five colours — with your title lettered onto them, or your own art imported, even for a single book. And a first “Your Edition” page that counts what is actually inside your build — totals, books, and the symbol legend, drawn from your real choices rather than an overstated boilerplate.

  5. In progress

    A standalone Geʽez Bible from the manuscripts

    Transcribing a complete Bible in classical Geʽez directly from the original manuscripts, witness by witness, with a faithful English back-translation after each book. It is slow, careful work. 1 and 2 Samuel are complete; the books of Kings are underway.

  6. Opening soon

    The first public release (Beta)

    Preparing the first public beta of the desktop program — free, complete, and open for everyone to try, with feedback warmly wanted. See downloads & releases →

Honestly, where it stands today

The vision above is real, and most of it is built — but it is only honest to say what is not finished. Today nine English study editions build end to end; the standalone Geʽez and Amharic Bibles are still being transcribed from the manuscripts and aren’t buildable yet. Note coverage is genuine but uneven across books and traditions, so an edition’s counts vary. AI-drafted commentary stays switched off unless you deliberately opt in. It is a complete, working program — not a finished one — and it’s free at exactly the stage it has reached.

With your support, what comes next

These are hopes, not commitments — undated, and conditional on the time and support to reach them. They’re listed so you can see where the work would go if there’s time and support for it.

  • All the manuscripts, rendered online — so the original pages can be read freely by anyone, not only transcribed into the text.
  • A standalone Amharic Bible — alongside the Geʽez, in its own right.
  • More public-domain source texts — deepening the storehouse further.
  • Public-domain audio — curated human-narrated readings (from LibriVox), embedded natively in the ebooks.
  • Broader reading plans — more ways to journey through the text.

These are undated hopes, not scheduled features. Some may arrive sooner, some later, and some may change. Whatever comes, the program stays completely free — a sponsorship helps keep it alive and growing (it pays for development time, manuscript digitization, and the hosting and domain for this site), but it never buys anyone a feature others don’t get.
