Your thoughts are welcome — a kind word, a suggestion, a confusing step, or a mistake you spotted. Corrections to an attribution, a Scripture reference, or a translation are especially welcome; this work aims to be honest, and you help keep it so.

The surest way to reach me is email. Write to gringo.boggy@yhwhyaway.com — it always reaches me, and never needs an account. You can also leave a threaded comment below, through GitHub Discussions; that needs a free GitHub account (email never does).

The comments are the one thing this site loads from another service — a frame from giscus.app and github.com, and only if you scroll down to them. Nothing else external is loaded, and the site runs no analytics of its own.
