Leave a note, or report a problem

Your thoughts are welcome — a kind word, a suggestion, a confusing step, or a mistake you spotted. Corrections to an attribution, a Scripture reference, or a translation are especially welcome; this work aims to be honest, and you help keep it so.

No GitHub account? Just email me. Write to gringo.boggy@yhwhyaway.com — it always reaches me, and it never needs an account. The comment box below is powered by GitHub Discussions and needs a free GitHub account; email never does.

The comment box is the one place this site loads anything from another service: it loads a frame from giscus.app and github.com, and only once you scroll down to it. This site runs no analytics of its own; the comment frame is GitHub’s and is governed by GitHub’s privacy terms.
