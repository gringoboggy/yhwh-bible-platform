መዝሙር ፲፬

The Psalms 14 · Geʽez & English

  1. Verse 1.፩

    መዝሙር ፡ ዘዳዊት ። እግዚኦ ፡ መኑ ፡ ይኀድር ፡ ውስተ ፡ ጽላሎትከ ፤ ወመኑ ፡ ያጸልል ፡ ውስተ ፡ ደብረ ፡ መቅድስከ ።

    A psalm of David. O Yahweh, who shall dwell in your tabernacle? And who shall abide on your holy mountain?

  2. Verse 2.፪

    ዘየሐውር ፡ በንጹሕ ፡ ወይገብር ፡ ጽድቀ ፤ ወዘይነብብ ፡ ጽድቀ ፡ በልቡ ።

    He who walks in purity and does righteousness; and who speaks truth in his heart;

  3. Verse 3.፫

    ወዘኢጓሕለወ ፡ በልሳኑ ፡ ወዘኢገብረ ፡ እኩየ ፡ ዲበ ፡ ቢጹ ፤ ወዘኢያጽአለ ፡ አዝማዲሁ ።

    and who has not deceived with his tongue, and has done no evil to his fellow, and has not reproached his kinsmen;

  4. Verse 4.፬

    ወዘምኑን ፡ በቅድሜሁ ፡ እኩይ ፡ ወዘያከብሮሙ ፡ ለፈራህያነ ፡ እግዚአብሔር ፤ ዘይምሕል ፡ ለቢጹ ፡ ወኢይሔሱ ።

    and in whose sight the evildoer is despised, but who honors those who fear Yahweh; who swears to his neighbor and does not break faith;

  5. Verse 5.፭

    ወዘኢለቅሐ ፡ ወርቆ ፡ በርዴ ፡ ወዘኢነሥአ ፡ ሕልያነ ፡ በላዕለ ፡ ንጹሕ ፤ ዘይገብር ፡ ከመዝ ፡ ኢይትሀወክ ፡ ለዓለም ።

    and who has not lent his money at interest, and has not taken a bribe against the innocent. He who does these things shall never be shaken.
