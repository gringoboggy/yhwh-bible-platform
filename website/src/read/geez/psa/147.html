መዝሙር ፻፵፯

The Psalms 147 · Geʽez & English

  1. Verse 1.፩

    ሀሌሉያ ። ትሴብሖ ፡ ኢየሩሳሌም ፡ ለእግዚአብሔር ፤ ሰብሕዮ ፡ ለአምላክኪ ፡ ጽዮን ።

    Hallelujah. Praise Yahweh, O Jerusalem; praise your God, O Zion.

  2. Verse 2.፪

    እስመ ፡ አጽንዐ ፡ መናስግተ ፡ ኆኃቲኪ ፤ ወባረኮሙ ፡ ለውሉድኪ ፡ በውስቴትኪ ።

    For he has strengthened the bars of your gates; and he has blessed your children within you.

  3. Verse 3.፫

    ወረሰየ ፡ ሰላመ ፡ ለበሓውርትኪ ፤ ወአጽገበኪ ፡ ቄቅሐ ፡ ስርናይ ።

    And he has set peace in your borders; and he has filled you with the finest of wheat.

  4. Verse 4.፬

    ዘይፌኑ ፡ ቃሎ ፡ ለምድር ፤ ወፍጡነ ፡ ይረውጽ ፡ ነቢቡ ።

    Who sends his word to the earth; and swiftly his utterance runs.

  5. Verse 5.፭

    ዘይሁብ ፡ በረደ ፡ ከመ ፡ ፀምር ፤ ወይዘርዎ ፡ ለጊሜ ፡ ከመ ፡ ሐመድ ።

    Who gives snow like wool; and scatters the mist like ashes.

  6. Verse 6.፮

    ወያወርድ ፡ በረደ ፡ ከመ ፡ ፍተታት ፤ መኑ ፡ ይትቃወሞ ፡ ለቍሩ ።

    And he sends down hail like morsels; who can stand before his cold?

  7. Verse 7.፯

    ይፌኑ ፡ ቃሎ ፡ ወይመስዎ ፤ ያነፍኅ ፡ መንፈሶ ፡ ወያውሕዝ ፡ ማያተ ።

    He sends his word and melts them; he makes his wind to blow, and the waters flow.

  8. Verse 8.፰

    ዘነገረ ፡ ቃሎ ፡ ለያዕቆብ ፤ ፍትሖ ፡ ወኵነኔሁ ፡ ለእስራኤል ።

    Who declared his word to Jacob; his statutes and his judgments to Israel.

  9. Verse 9.፱

    ወኢገብረ ፡ ከማሁ ፡ ለባዕዳን ፡ አሕዛብ ፤ ወኢነገሮሙ ፡ ፍትሖ ።

    He has not done so for any other nations; and he has not made known to them his judgments.

Geʽez transcribed from the manuscripts; the English is a literal back-translation — a reading aid, not a replacement for the Geʽez. Free to read, copy, and share.
