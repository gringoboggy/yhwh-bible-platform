መዝሙር ፻፶

The Psalms 150 · Geʽez & English

  1. Verse 1.፩

    ሀሌሉያ ። ሰብሕዎ ፡ ለእግዚአብሔር ፡ በቅዱሳኑ ፤ ሰብሕዎ ፡ በጽንዐ ፡ ኀይሉ ።

    Hallelujah. Praise Yahweh in his holy ones; praise him in the strength of his power.

  2. Verse 2.፪

    ሰብሕዎ ፡ በክሂሎቱ ፤ ሰብሕዎ ፡ በከመ ፡ ብዝኀ ፡ ዕበዩ ።

    Praise him for his mighty acts; praise him according to the abundance of his greatness.

  3. Verse 3.፫

    ሰብሕዎ ፡ በቃለ ፡ ቀርን ፤ ሰብሕዎ ፡ በመዝሙር ፡ ወበመሰንቆ ።

    Praise him with the sound of the horn; praise him with the harp and the lyre.

  4. Verse 4.፬

    ሰብሕዎ ፡ በከበሮ ፡ ወበትፍሥሕት ፤ ሰብሕዎ ፡ በአውታር ፡ ወበዕንዚራ ።

    Praise him with the timbrel and with dancing; praise him with strings and with the pipe.

  5. Verse 5.፭

    ሰብሕዎ ፡ በጸናጽል ፡ ዘሠናይ ፡ ቃሉ ፤ ሰብሕዎ ፡ በጸናጽል ፡ ወበይባቤ ።

    Praise him with cymbals of beautiful sound; praise him with cymbals and with a shout of joy.

  6. Verse 6.፮

    ኵሉ ፡ ነፍስ ፡ ይሴብሖ ፡ ለእግዚአብሔር ።

    Let every soul praise Yahweh.

Geʽez transcribed from the manuscripts; the English is a literal back-translation — a reading aid, not a replacement for the Geʽez. Free to read, copy, and share.
